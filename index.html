Range Gauge

by NoTech Studios

Point. Measure.
Know the distance.

Range Gauge uses your phone's camera and ARCore depth sensing to measure distance to any object in real time.

Download
Android 7.0+  ·  ARCore required  ·  Free  ·  No ads
🎯

Live Distance Readout

ARCore depth sensing measures distance to the target in real time, updating 10 times per second.

👁

Night Vision Mode

GPU-accelerated green-tint night vision for low-light conditions — no flash required.

🔍

Digital Zoom

Zoom from 1× to 8× with on-screen controls. Distance measurement always stays locked to center.

🌍

Any Orientation

Works in portrait or landscape. The UI adapts and ARCore stays in sync automatically.

How to install

  1. Download the app to your Android device.
  2. Open your device Settings → Security and enable Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
  3. Open the downloaded APK file and tap Install.
  4. Launch Range Gauge and grant camera permission when prompted.
